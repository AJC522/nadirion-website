03 About Nadirion

Founded by practitioners, named for a point of reference.

The nadir is the point directly beneath an observer: the fixed reference from which everything above is measured. Nadirion was established by cybersecurity professionals with backgrounds in federal service, defense consulting, and critical infrastructure protection, and that grounding shapes how we work: methodical, discreet, and rooted in operational reality rather than theory.

We deliver accurate, timely analysis that helps clients understand and manage their security risk. Decisions get made on evidence, not guesswork. The same standard carries into the software we write and the platforms we build.

Accuracy

Findings you can act on and defend, backed by rigorous methodology.

Timeliness

Intelligence and analysis delivered while it still matters.

Discretion

Sensitive engagements handled with the judgment they require.
