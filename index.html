04 How It's Built

Tools you can defend

Whatever modules you choose, they follow the same rules: say why, ask before changing anything, and leave a way back.

Offline-first

Most modules never touch the network. Enrichment through VirusTotal, AbuseIPDB, Hybrid Analysis, or SerpAPI uses your own keys, held in the OS keyring.

Explainable

Scores come with their reasons: rule-level rationale for processes, an ATT&CK technique on every email finding, evidence tiers for AI analysis.

Read-only by default

Forensic modules inspect and report. Anything that changes a host asks first in the window and needs --yes in the console.

Reversible

Secure Host snapshots the firewall before isolating a machine. Startup Manager and Malware Scan remediation can be undone.

Verified

USB ISO Burner checks checksums before writing and reads back after. Triage kits ship a SHA-256 manifest. Updates are hash-checked.

Cross-platform

Windows, macOS, and Linux. Where a module behaves differently on one of them, its catalog entry says so.
