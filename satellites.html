06 Focus Area In development

CubeSats you can print, fly, and revise

Conventional smallsat structures wait on tooling. Ours do not. By additively manufacturing the primary structure and bus components, a design change can be printed, integrated, and back on the bench in the time a machined part spends in a queue. That matters most where it usually hurts most: keeping a constellation current instead of frozen at the date it was ordered.

01 Approach

Why additive, specifically

Printing a satellite is not the point. Collapsing the distance between a design change and a flight article is the point. Everything below follows from that.

01

Iteration measured in days

A structural revision goes from CAD to a physical article without a tooling cycle. Over a constellation program that compounds: the fifth batch can carry lessons the first batch taught, instead of repeating a design frozen years earlier.

02

Geometry a mill cannot cut

Integrated harness runs, conformal radiators, and lattice structures that put mass only where load actually goes. Fewer fasteners and fewer joints means fewer of the interfaces that fail on vibration.

03

Unit cost that permits risk

When a bus is cheap enough to lose, a constellation can fly newer technology sooner and accept a shorter design life. That is a different economic model from a single exquisite spacecraft, and it needs different manufacturing.

04

A supply chain you can see

Printing in house removes a tier of vendors from the critical path and from the threat surface. We treat the design files and the print process as things worth protecting, because they are.

Additively manufactured primary structure with printed bus components and standard rail interfaces for common deployers.

Payloads

Earth observation optics and RF sensing packages, with a hosted payload option on the larger buses.

Ground segment

Tasking, downlink handling, and processing built by our software team on the same evidence store as the toolkit.

Security

Command authentication, signed firmware, and supply chain review treated as requirements from the first print.

03 Principles

What carries across every platform

The same three commitments govern our maritime and aerial programs.

Additive first

Short build cycles keep the fleet current instead of frozen at the date it was ordered.

Sensor to decision

These are collection assets. What they gather lands in the same command view that runs our cyber work.

Hardened by default

Firmware, command links, and supply chain are treated as attack surface from the first print.
