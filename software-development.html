01 Capability

What we build

Five areas, all of them things we have shipped for ourselves before shipping them for a client. If your project does not fit one of these, say so on the scoping call and we will tell you honestly whether we are the right shop.

B-01

Mission & Enterprise Applications

Internal tools, operator consoles, case management, and workflow systems for teams whose work does not fit an off-the-shelf product. Built for the people who will sit in front of them eight hours a day.

  • Web applications and operator interfaces
  • Case, workflow, and evidence management
  • Role-based access and audit logging
  • Accessibility and keyboard-first design
B-02

Data Pipelines & Analytics

Getting telemetry, imagery, and log data from where it is produced to where a decision gets made, without losing provenance along the way.

  • Ingest, normalization, and enrichment
  • Streaming and batch processing
  • Search, correlation, and reporting layers
  • Retention and provenance controls
B-03

Integration & Automation

Connecting the systems you already paid for. API work, connector development, and the automation that removes a manual step somebody currently does at 2am.

  • API design, development, and documentation

  • Workflow and response automation
  • Legacy system interfaces

Security Tooling

Detection content, analysis utilities, and internal platforms for security teams. This is the work our own toolkit came out of, so it is well travelled ground.

02 Method

How we build it

A secure development lifecycle that produces working software on a schedule, with the security work spread across the whole build instead of piled up at the end.

  1. 01

    Threat model first

    Before the first commit we agree on what the system protects, who wants it, and what an acceptable failure looks like. That document drives the architecture.

  2. 02

    Build in short cycles

    Working software in front of you every couple of weeks. Requirements change, and finding that out at week four costs less than finding out at month six.

  3. 03

    Test like an attacker

    Unit and integration tests are the floor. Our own security practice reviews the build, and abuse cases get tested alongside the happy path.

  4. 04

    Hand over cleanly

    You get the source, the build pipeline, the deployment runbook, and the documentation. No dependency on us to keep the lights on unless you want one.
