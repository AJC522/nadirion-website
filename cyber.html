03 Engagement

How an engagement runs

Predictable from the first call. You know what happens at each stage, what we need from you, and what lands on your desk at the end of it.

  1. 01

    Scoping call

    Thirty to sixty minutes on your environment, your constraints, and what would count as a good outcome. No cost, no obligation.

  2. 02

    Baseline

    We deploy the toolkit in your chosen mode and establish what is actually there: assets, identities, exposure, and existing detection coverage.

  3. 03

    Operate

    Assessment, hunting, emulation, or response work runs against that baseline, with a standing check-in so nothing lands as a surprise.

  4. 04

    Brief and hand off

    Findings, prioritized remediation, and a command view your team keeps. Where you want it, we train your people to run the toolkit themselves.

04 Deployment

Three ways to run it

Where your data has to stay is a requirement, not a preference. Each mode is a supported configuration with its own hardening guide.

On-premises

Runs inside your perimeter on hardware you control. Evidence never leaves your estate.

Air-gapped

Full capability with no outbound path. Intelligence updates arrive through a controlled transfer process.

Cloud tenancy

Deployed into your own cloud account, under your identity provider and your logging.
