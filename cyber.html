03 Engagement

How an engagement runs

Predictable from the first call. You know what happens at each stage, what we need from you, and what lands on your desk at the end of it.

  1. 01

    Scoping call

    Thirty to sixty minutes on your environment, your constraints, and what would count as a good outcome. No cost, no obligation.

  2. 02

    Baseline

    We set up Nadirion Desktop with the modules the work calls for and establish what is actually there: assets, identities, exposure, and existing detection coverage.

  3. 03

    Operate

    Assessment, hunting, emulation, or response work runs against that baseline, with a standing check-in so nothing lands as a surprise.

  4. 04

    Brief and hand off

    Findings, prioritized remediation, and a written brief your team keeps. Where you want it, we train your people to run Nadirion Desktop themselves.

04 Deployment

Where the work runs

Where your data has to stay is a requirement, not a preference. Nadirion Desktop is built to work inside it.

On the workstation

Installed per user on Windows, macOS, or Linux. Settings and case data stay in a local folder; API keys stay in the OS keyring.

Offline & air-gapped

Most modules need no network at all. Enrichment through outside services is opt-in and uses your own keys.

On the affected host

Triage Kit USB carries read-only collectors and a portable console to a machine with nothing installed, and writes results back to the kit.
