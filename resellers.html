02 Certification

What “certified” will mean

Nadirion builds software that inspects hosts, isolates machines, and writes to drives. The people who sell it should understand it and be careful about whom they sell it to.

C-01

Product certification

Training on Nadirion Desktop and every module category, followed by an assessment: which modules suit which kinds of work, what each one reads or changes, and where its limits are. [VERIFY: whether the training and assessment exist today or are still being prepared.]

  • Desktop, console, and licensing
  • Every module category, with platform notes
  • Matching modules to a buyer's work
C-02

A reseller ID

Issued only once you are certified and the agreement is signed. It goes on your quotes and invoices, and buyers can confirm it with Nadirion before they pay.

  • One ID per certified reseller
  • Verifiable by any buyer
  • Valid while certification is current
C-03

Know your customer

Certified resellers screen their buyers and decline any sale that would violate export-control or sanctions regulations or that appears intended for misuse.

  • Buyer identity confirmed before sale
  • Export-control and sanctions screening
  • Questionable requests escalated to Nadirion
C-04

Accurate representation

Represent what the product does, and nothing more. Module claims come from Nadirion's own documentation, including each module's stated limitation and the free tools that cover similar ground.

  • Capabilities described as documented
  • Platform limits disclosed up front
  • No promises on Nadirion's behalf beyond the agreement

06 Questions

Reseller FAQ

Anything else, ask us directly.

How many certified resellers are there?

[VERIFY: e.g. “None yet. The program is new, and we are reviewing the first applications.”]

Can an individual become a reseller?

Yes. The individual track exists for consultants, responders, trainers, and other practitioners who sell under their own name. You complete certification yourself and hold the reseller agreement personally.

What does "certified" tell a buyer?

That the reseller completed Nadirion's product training and assessment, signed the reseller agreement, and holds a current reseller ID that the buyer can confirm with us. It is not a third-party certification.

Do I need a security background?

You need to understand your buyers' work well enough to match modules to it. Certification covers the product, and the application asks about the experience you bring.

Who supports my customers after the sale?

You are their first point of contact. Nadirion answers your questions by email and takes on the product issues you escalate. [VERIFY: support level offered to resellers.]

Where am I allowed to sell?

Your territory and the markets you serve are agreed in your reseller agreement. Tell us about them when you apply.
