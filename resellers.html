02 Certification

What "certified" means

Nadirion makes software that reads hosts, isolates machines, and writes drives. The people who sell it should understand it, and should care who they sell it to.

C-01

Product certification

Training on Nadirion Desktop and every module category, followed by an assessment. You'll know which modules fit which work, what each one reads or changes, and where its limits are.

  • Desktop, console, and licensing
  • Every module category, with platform notes
  • Matching modules to a buyer's work
C-02

A reseller ID

Issued once you are certified and the agreement is signed. It goes on your quotes and invoices, and buyers can confirm it with Nadirion before they pay.

  • One ID per certified reseller
  • Verifiable by any buyer
  • Valid while certification is current
C-03

Know your customer

Certified resellers screen their buyers and decline sales that would break export-control or sanctions rules, or that look intended for misuse.

  • Buyer identity confirmed before sale
  • Export-control and sanctions screening
  • Questionable requests escalated to Nadirion
C-04

Accurate representation

Sell what the product does, not more. Module claims come from Nadirion's own documentation, including each module's stated limits.

  • Capabilities described as documented
  • Platform limits disclosed up front
  • No promises on Nadirion's behalf beyond the agreement

06 Questions

Reseller FAQ

Anything else, ask us directly.

Can an individual really become a reseller?

Yes. The individual track exists for consultants, responders, trainers, and other practitioners who sell under their own name. You complete certification yourself and hold the reseller agreement personally.

What does "certified" tell a buyer?

That the reseller completed Nadirion's product training and assessment, signed the reseller agreement, and holds a current reseller ID that the buyer can confirm with us.

Do I need a security background?

You need to understand your buyers' work well enough to fit modules to it. Certification covers the product; the application asks about the experience you bring.

Who supports my customers after the sale?

You are their first point of contact. Nadirion backs you with partner support and takes on the product issues you escalate.

Where am I allowed to sell?

Your territory and the markets you serve are agreed in your reseller agreement. Tell us about them when you apply.
