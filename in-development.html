02 How It Gets Here

From research to release

Every new offering moves through the same four stages. We state clearly which stage each one is in, so nothing is sold before it exists.

01 · Research

Is it worth building?

We look at a problem and whether a new product or service would solve it well before committing to build it.

02 · In development

Shown, not yet sold

Once it is being built and can be described accurately, it is listed as in development, as the module catalog lists Intelligence Map and Investigation Queue today. It follows the same rules as Nadirion Desktop: offline where possible, scores that show their reasoning, and changes that can be undone.

03 · Preview

On sale, marked Preview

A newly released module, or one with limited real-world testing, goes on sale marked Preview, priced as the price list states, until it has more use behind it.

04 · Available

On sale, plainly stated

After that, it is listed with everything else we sell, with its price and its main limitation stated.
