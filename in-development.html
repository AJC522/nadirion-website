02 How It Gets Here

From research to release

Everything new moves through the same four stages. We'll be clear about which stage anything is in, so nothing is sold before it exists.

01 · Research

Is it worth building?

We look at problems our users face and test whether a new product or service would actually solve them before committing to build it.

02 · Development

Built to the same standard

What passes research is built and tested against real workflows, held to the rules Nadirion Desktop follows: offline-first where it can be, explainable, and reversible.

03 · Preview

Shown, not yet sold

Work that is close to ready is listed as in development, the way the module catalog lists Intelligence Map and Investigation Queue today.

04 · Available

On sale, plainly stated

When it's ready, it goes on sale and is listed alongside Nadirion Desktop, with pricing and availability stated as clearly as everything else we sell.
