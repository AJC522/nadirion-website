Website privacy

This page describes what the nadirion.us website does with information. It covers this website only, not Nadirion Desktop or our services.

No cookies, analytics, or trackers

This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts. Its fonts, scripts, and images are all served from nadirion.us itself, so loading a page makes no requests to other websites.

Your module selection

When you add modules to a license in the catalog, the site saves the list in your browser's local storage under the name nadirion.license.modules, so it is still there on the contact page. The same list is carried in the address of the Request a Quote link. It stays on your device unless you send it to us in a quote request. To remove it, use Clear in the license tray or clear this site's data in your browser.

Your motion setting

If you pause the animation on the home page, the site remembers that choice in your browser's local storage under the name nadirion.motion. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes it.

Contact and reseller forms

The forms do not send anything on their own. Submitting one opens your email app with a message addressed to contact@nadirion.us, and it reaches us only if you send it.

Email contact@nadirion.us.
